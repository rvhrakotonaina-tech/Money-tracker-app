Privacy Policy

Money Tracker

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Money Tracker, a personal finance management application designed to help you track your income, expenses, budgets, loans, debts, and other personal financial activities.

Your privacy is important to us. Money Tracker is designed with an offline-first approach, allowing you to manage your financial records on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Money Tracker, how it is stored and processed, and what choices you have regarding your information.

By using Money Tracker, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the application, Money Tracker may handle the following information:

Personal financial information

You may enter financial information such as:

  • Income and expenses.
  • Transaction amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Budgets and spending records.
  • Loans, debts, repayment amounts, and repayment history.
  • Other information you choose to enter into the application.

This information is used to provide the application's financial tracking and management features.

Information stored on your device

Money Tracker uses a local database to store financial records on your device. This allows you to access and manage your records without requiring a permanent internet connection. Financial information entered into the application is intended to remain on your device unless you use a feature or third-party service that transmits it elsewhere.

Device and technical information

The application or its underlying libraries may process technical information necessary for the application to operate. Depending on the components included in the installed version, this may include diagnostic information, crash reports, or other technical data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information handled by Money Tracker is used to:

  • Record and organize income and expenses.
  • Calculate balances and summarize financial activity.
  • Help you manage budgets, loans, debts, and repayments.
  • Display transaction histories and financial charts.
  • Provide AI-assisted functionality where available.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the application's functionality.

Money Tracker is intended for personal financial organization and tracking.

4. Local Storage and Offline Functionality

Money Tracker uses a local database based on Room and SQLite to manage financial records on your device.

Core financial tracking features are designed to work offline. Your financial records do not need to be uploaded to a remote server for ordinary local tracking functionality.

However, internet access, external communications, or third-party processing may occur if a particular feature or included software component requires them. Any such behavior should be assessed against the actual version of the application you install.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device and maintaining your own backups of important financial information.

5. Artificial Intelligence Features

Money Tracker includes an AI-assisted feature designed to help users interact with financial information and obtain relevant assistance.

The application is designed to support on-device AI processing using a locally running language model. Where a feature operates entirely on your device, the information supplied to that feature is processed locally rather than sent to an external AI service.

The actual handling of information depends on the AI model, application configuration, and any services used by the particular feature. If a future or current feature sends information to an external service, that processing must be disclosed accordingly.

AI-generated responses may be inaccurate or incomplete. You should verify important calculations and decisions independently.

Money Tracker is a personal finance tracking tool and does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Money Tracker is designed to keep your financial records under your control on your device.

We do not intend to sell your personal financial records to third parties.

Information may be processed or disclosed where necessary to operate features that you choose to use, comply with applicable legal obligations, protect the security of the application, or address misuse.

Any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, crash-reporting services, advertising components, or external APIs included in the application may handle information according to their actual functionality and applicable privacy policies.

The information shared, if any, depends on the components and features present in the version you use.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial records stored locally remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application or its stored data.

Deleting the application or clearing its data may permanently remove locally stored transactions and other financial records. Information exported or backed up separately may remain in those locations until you delete it.

If information is processed by a third-party service, its retention and deletion may also be governed by that service's policies.

You should export or back up important records before uninstalling the application or clearing its data.

8. Data Security

We aim to protect the information handled by Money Tracker through appropriate application design and data-handling practices.

Because financial records may be stored locally, their security also depends on your device's security settings, operating system, and physical access controls.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend keeping your device updated and using appropriate screen-lock and device-security measures.

9. Children's Privacy

Money Tracker is a general personal finance application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information in violation of applicable privacy laws. If you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

10. Your Choices and Control

You can control the financial information you enter into Money Tracker by managing your records within the application.

Depending on the features available in your version, you may be able to edit or delete transactions and other financial records, or export information for your own use.

You can also stop using the application at any time. Before uninstalling it, consider exporting or backing up records that you wish to preserve.

11. Third-Party Services

Money Tracker may depend on third-party software libraries or services for certain functionality.

If such services are included, their processing of information is subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. The inclusion and behavior of these services may change as the application is updated.

Users should consult the relevant third-party policies where applicable.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

When an updated policy is published, the date at the beginning of this document will be revised. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
